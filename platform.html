Flagship application

SFT Molecular Platform.

An integrated computational platform for molecular property prediction, scientific validation, candidate prioritisation and reproducible research workflows.

The platform brings shared molecular representations, endpoint-aware prediction modules, controlled validation and scientific reporting into one connected environment. It is designed to help researchers move from a molecular question to reviewable computational evidence without treating each prediction as an isolated result.

One connected environment

More than six separate prediction tools.

The SFT Molecular Platform is an integrated scientific software environment that combines mathematical modelling, computational descriptors, machine learning, validation controls and reproducible reporting within a shared architecture.

Its purpose is to reduce fragmentation across molecular prediction workflows. Rather than moving manually between unrelated packages, researchers can work through common input handling, shared molecular representations, endpoint-specific models and consistent scientific outputs.

The platform is being developed for computational researchers, universities, biotechnology and pharmaceutical organisations, contract research teams and scientific software users who need prediction tools that remain traceable, benchmarkable and suitable for integration into wider research workflows.

ResearchersSingle-molecule analysis, hypothesis development and candidate comparison.
Research teamsBatch workflows, shared reporting and reproducible computational studies.

Integrated prediction modules

Six endpoint technologies within one platform.

Each module addresses a distinct molecular question while using shared platform services for input handling, descriptors, model execution, reporting and audit.

SFT-logP

Lipophilicity prediction.

Estimates partition behaviour through molecular descriptors designed to capture hydrophobic, polar and structural contributions.

Primary output
Predicted logP and associated reporting artefacts
Research use
Compound comparison, permeability context and formulation-oriented analysis

SFT-Solubility

Why the platform is different

Computational prioritisation before experimental commitment.

The platform is designed to support experimental decision-making, not replace it. Its value lies in helping teams organise evidence, compare candidates and focus laboratory effort.
