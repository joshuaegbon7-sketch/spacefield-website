Spacefield Transformation Technologies is a UK company developing mathematical and computational systems for molecular science and research-led discovery.

Our direction

We build computational foundations capable of supporting more disciplined molecular modelling, prediction, prioritisation and reporting. The SFT Molecular Platform is our first flagship application and a foundation for future scientific computing technologies.

Mission

To engineer rigorous computational technologies that transform how scientific problems are represented, analysed and validated.

Identity

Scientific. Mathematical. Computational. Transformational. Trustworthy.
