How we can engage

Enquiries organised around clear scientific and technical goals.

Different conversations require different information. Selecting the closest enquiry type helps establish the right scope from the beginning.

R

Research collaboration

Joint methodology work, academic studies, independent validation, dataset collaboration and publication-focused projects.

Useful context: research question, collaborators, available data and intended outputs.
V

Experimental validation

Targeted laboratory testing of computational predictions, candidate rankings or molecular-property estimates.

Useful context: assay type, target, compounds, controls, timelines and laboratory capability.
P

Platform evaluation

Private technical review, pilot access, workflow assessment, benchmarking or due-diligence evaluation.

Useful context: evaluation objective, data volume, endpoints, security and success criteria.
API

Software integration

API, SDK, batch processing, reporting, deployment and integration with existing scientific workflows.

Useful context: preferred interface, infrastructure, scale, compliance and support needs.
B

Business and licensing

Commercial evaluation, licensing models, pilot projects, strategic partnerships and enterprise deployment.

Useful context: organisation, intended users, deployment model and decision timeline.
A

Advisory and specialist support

Scientific, legal, intellectual-property, regulatory, software-engineering and commercial advisory relationships.

Useful context: area of expertise, proposed contribution and expected engagement format.
Useful information to include

Help us understand the project before the first discussion.

A concise, structured enquiry usually leads to a faster and more useful response.

Please avoid sending highly confidential molecular structures, unpublished datasets or sensitive partner information in an initial message unless an appropriate confidentiality arrangement is already in place.

01
Your organisation

Name, sector, location and the role of the person making the enquiry.

02
Scientific objective

The molecular, biological or computational question you want to address.

03
Requested work

Prediction, benchmarking, platform evaluation, experimental validation or software integration.

04
Available materials

Approximate dataset size, molecular formats, target information and existing measurements.

05
Timeline and outcome

Expected start date, decision points, deliverables and any fixed deadlines.

06
Confidentiality requirements

Whether an NDA, data agreement or restricted review process may be required.

Scientific and communication boundaries

Clear expectations protect both the research and the relationship.

Spacefield will not present computational predictions as experimentally established facts or accept work that requires unsupported therapeutic, clinical or regulatory conclusions.

Appropriate

Suitable enquiry examples

  • Evaluate a defined set of compounds against selected platform endpoints
  • Compare a Spacefield module with an agreed baseline on a controlled dataset
  • Design a laboratory study to test specific computational predictions
  • Discuss API or SDK integration into an existing research workflow
  • Explore a research collaboration with clear scientific outputs
Requires care

Matters needing additional controls

  • Confidential molecules, targets or unpublished partner data
  • Claims involving therapeutic efficacy, safety or clinical decisions
  • Regulated or security-sensitive research environments
  • Requests involving licensed datasets or third-party intellectual property
  • Commercial use before licensing and validation status are defined
