Accessibility Statement

Spacefield Transformation Technologies Ltd is committed to providing a website that is accessible and usable for as many people as reasonably possible.

Effective date: 7 August 2026  ·  Last reviewed: 7 August 2026

Our commitment

Spacefield Transformation Technologies Ltd is committed to providing a website that is accessible and usable for as many people as reasonably possible.

We aim to ensure that visitors can access our scientific, technical and corporate information regardless of the device, browser or assistive technology they use. Accessibility is considered throughout the development of our website, and we continue to review and improve accessibility as the website evolves.

Accessibility features

  • Semantic HTML with a logical heading hierarchy.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation.
  • Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
  • Readable typography and sufficient colour contrast.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images where appropriate.
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences where implemented.
  • Consistent navigation and page structure.
  • Scalable text using standard browser zoom controls.

Browser and device compatibility

The website is designed to work with current versions of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox and Apple Safari. It is also designed to function across desktop computers, tablets and modern smartphones.

Assistive technologies

We aim to maintain compatibility with commonly used assistive technologies, including screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, browser zoom and magnification tools, and operating system accessibility features.

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing process. As Spacefield develops new website features, scientific content and software resources, we will continue reviewing accessibility, usability and compatibility in line with recognised good practice.

Known limitations

Although we aim to provide an accessible website, some content may not yet achieve the same level of accessibility as newer sections of the site. Where issues are identified, we will seek to address them in future updates where reasonably practicable.

Feedback

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us.

Future improvements

Accessibility will remain an integral part of our development process. Future improvements may include enhanced keyboard navigation, additional accessibility testing, improved compatibility with assistive technologies and continued alignment with recognised accessibility guidance.
