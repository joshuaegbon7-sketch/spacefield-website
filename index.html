Computational science for molecular discovery.

Spacefield Transformation Technologies Ltd is a computational science company developing advanced software platforms for molecular science, drug discovery and scientific research. Our technologies combine mathematical modelling, scientific computing and molecular prediction to support molecular research and experimental decision-making. Our flagship application, the SFT Molecular Platform, helps researchers prioritise candidate molecules for experimental validation and further investigation.

About Spacefield

Scientific technology built for rigorous computational research.

Spacefield Transformation Technologies Ltd is a UK-based scientific technology company developing advanced computational platforms for molecular modelling, scientific computing and AI-assisted scientific discovery.

We combine mathematical modelling, mechanism-informed molecular representations, software engineering and machine learning to create traceable computational systems that support research, prioritisation and experimental decision-making.
