Company overview

A computational science company built around research, engineering and scientific evidence.

Spacefield develops computational technologies that connect scientific reasoning to usable software. The company is focused on molecular science, drug discovery research and broader scientific computing, with an emphasis on transparent methods, reproducible workflows and clearly defined scientific boundaries.

Our flagship application, the SFT Molecular Platform, brings together shared molecular representations, endpoint-specific prediction modules, validation context and scientific reporting within one connected computational environment.

Spacefield is being developed as a broader scientific technology company rather than around a single product. The same computational and engineering foundations can support future software platforms, research tools and scientific applications as the company grows.

Mission and vision

Scientific ambition with engineering discipline.

Spacefield exists to advance molecular science through rigorous computational methods, scientific software engineering and reproducible research technologies.

Mission

To advance molecular science through rigorous computational methods, scientific software engineering and reproducible research technologies.

We pursue this mission by combining mathematical modelling, scientific computing, computational molecular methods, controlled validation and maintainable software engineering.

To build trusted computational infrastructure that helps researchers move from complex molecular questions to reviewable evidence and better-informed experimental decisions.

Company story

From theoretical research to a scientific software company.

Spacefield emerged from a programme of independent mathematical and computational research that gradually developed into a broader technology effort.

01
Foundational research

Mathematical and computational ideas

Early work focused on theoretical formulations, operators, structure, transformation and the search for representations that could be made computationally explicit.

02
Molecular extension

From theory to molecular representation

The research expanded toward molecular descriptors, interaction geometry, structure-aware computation and the possibility of using shared representations across multiple molecular endpoints.

03
Scientific software

Building specialised prediction modules

Separate technologies were developed for pKa, logP, solubility, blood–brain barrier permeability, ADMET properties and protein–ligand binding.

04
Platform integration

The SFT Molecular Platform

The individual modules began to converge into an integrated environment with shared inputs, reporting, interfaces, validation records and reproducibility controls.

05
Company formation

Spacefield Transformation Technologies Ltd

The company was established to protect, develop, validate and ultimately deploy these computational technologies through research partnerships, pilot projects and commercial scientific software.

How we operate

Research, engineering and responsible deployment.

Our operating model is designed to preserve scientific rigour while moving toward usable products and sustainable collaboration.

01

Research before claims

New ideas are tested against data, baselines and defined evaluation plans before they are presented as platform capabilities.

02

Freeze before release

Important models and software states are preserved through versioning, manifests, hashes and release records.

03

Separate evidence from aspiration

Current validated capabilities are distinguished from future modules, roadmap items and long-term research goals.

04

Protect intellectual property responsibly

Scientific transparency is balanced with the need to protect proprietary code, models, data and unpublished implementation details.

05

Collaborate through defined scope

Research and industry engagements should use clear objectives, data responsibilities, success criteria and access conditions.

06

Design for deployment

Research software is progressively prepared for stable APIs, batch workflows, reporting, documentation and support.

Governance and responsibility

Building credibility into the company as well as the software.

Spacefield's public presence, collaborations and future commercial activity should be governed by clear scientific, legal and ethical boundaries.

§

Legal identity

Spacefield Transformation Technologies Ltd is registered in England and Wales under Company No. 17368270.

IP

Intellectual property

Proprietary software, model artefacts, documentation and research outputs are managed with appropriate protection and access controls.

R

Responsible research

Computational predictions are presented as research evidence and not as proof of efficacy, safety or regulatory suitability.

D

Data responsibility

Datasets and partner information should be used under applicable licences, agreements, privacy requirements and security controls.

V

Validation discipline

Development, validation and locked testing are kept conceptually separate to reduce misleading performance claims.

C

Clear communication

Website, research and commercial materials should distinguish present capability, planned development and scientific uncertainty.
